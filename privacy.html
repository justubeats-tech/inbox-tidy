Inbox Tidy privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026. Inbox Tidy is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own email account.

Who uses it

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public, and the only Google account it accesses is the owner's.

What data it accesses

  • Message details: sender, reply-to address, subject, date, size, labels, and the list-unsubscribe and sender-authentication headers. Message contents are not requested or read.
  • Mail settings: the filters it creates to label new messages.
  • The signed-in account's email address and basic profile, to confirm which account is signed in.

Permissions it asks Google for

  • Read, label and move mail to Trash (gmail.modify). This permission cannot permanently delete mail.
  • Manage mail filters (gmail.settings.basic). Mail forwarding is not part of this permission.

How the data is used

Only to sort the owner's own mail into labels, create mail filters, and move promotional email from senders the owner approved to the Trash, as described on the home page. The data is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Where the data is stored

  • The sign-in token is stored encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • A cache of message details (no message contents) and a log of label and Trash changes, kept so changes can be undone, are stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's personal cloud storage.

Sharing

The data is not shared with or sent to anyone else. When the owner reviews which senders to clean up, they may use an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) on their own computer to help read lists of senders and subjects; that use is covered by Anthropic's own terms.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept only while the owner uses the tool. The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local data from their computer.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
