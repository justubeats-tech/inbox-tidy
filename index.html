Inbox Tidy

A personal tool that its owner uses to keep their own email inbox tidy. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else signs in to it.

What it does

  • Sorts the owner's incoming mail into labels such as Updates, Social and Food & Drink Offers.
  • Moves promotional email from senders the owner has approved to the Trash, where it stays recoverable for 30 days. It cannot permanently delete anything.
  • Creates mail filters that label new messages as they arrive.
  • Runs once a week on the owner's own computer and writes a summary for the owner.

What it looks at

Message details such as sender, subject, date and labels, plus mail filter settings. It does not read the contents of messages.
